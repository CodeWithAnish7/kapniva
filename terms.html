About KapNiva

KapNiva is a marketplace that connects customers with independent local clothing sellers. Products are sold and delivered by the sellers. KapNiva provides the platform.

Accounts

  • You must give correct information and keep your mobile number secure.
  • One mobile number can be used as both a customer and a seller account.
  • You are responsible for activity on your account.

Sellers

  • Sellers must be verified before they can sell. Verification needs real shop details, a shop photo and a supporting document.
  • Sellers must list accurate products, prices and stock, and must not sell prohibited or counterfeit goods.
  • Important shop details (shop name, address, owner, GST, mobile number and shop image) can change only after KapNiva approves a request.
  • One shop can have only one seller account. Duplicate shop registrations are not allowed.

Orders and prices

  • The price and delivery charge shown when you place an order are fixed for that order.
  • Order quantity cannot be changed after the order is placed. Cancel and place a new order if needed.
  • Delivery is confirmed with a one-time delivery code. Share the code only when you have received your order.

Ratings and reviews

  • Only customers who have received a product can rate it, and each product in an order can be rated once.
  • Reviews must be honest and must not contain abuse, threats, personal details or spam.
  • Your name and review are shown publicly on the product page. KapNiva may remove reviews that break these rules.

Cancellations

You can cancel an order before the seller accepts it. After that, contact support. Sellers can cancel an order, for example when a product is unavailable, and must give a reason. See the Cancellation & Refund policy.

Misuse

We may limit, suspend or ban accounts for fake orders, spam, fraud, fake shops or misuse of the service. A suspended user can ask for a review through support. Order history and necessary transaction records are not deleted.

Payments

Available payment options may change over time. Online payments are processed by a payment partner when available.

Liability

KapNiva is a platform and is not the seller of the products. We work to resolve problems between customers and sellers but are not responsible for sellers' products beyond what the law requires.
