Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about shopping on KapNiva.

Is the KapNiva app available?

The app is coming soon. This website is an information page until launch.

How do I become a seller?

Create a seller account in the app, submit your shop details and a photo of your shop, and our team verifies your shop before you can sell. See the seller page for details.

Can I cancel an order?

Yes, you can cancel before the seller accepts the order. After that, please contact support. Read our cancellation policy.

What is the delivery code?

It is a one-time code you share with the delivery person only when you have received your order. It protects you from orders marked delivered by mistake.

How do I contact KapNiva?

Write to us at kapniva@gmail.com or visit the contact page.
