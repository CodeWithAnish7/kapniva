Delete Your KapNiva Account

Last updated: November 2026

You can delete your KapNiva account at any time. KapNiva has two kinds of accounts, and they are deleted separately: your shopper account and your seller shop. Deleting one does not delete the other.

Delete your shopper account (in the app)

  1. Open the KapNiva app and log in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete my account.
  4. Read the message and confirm. Your account is deleted right away.

Delete your seller shop (in the app)

  1. Open the KapNiva app and log in as a seller.
  2. Open the side menu (drawer).
  3. Tap Delete my shop.
  4. Read the message and confirm. Your shop is deleted.

If your shop still has active orders, finish or cancel them first. The app will tell you if an active order is blocking deletion.

What is deleted

  • Shopper account: your name, delivery addresses, cart, wishlist and saved details, and the device tokens used for notifications.
  • Seller shop: your shop profile, products and product images, shop photos, verification documents and notification device tokens.

What may be kept

  • Order and transaction records are kept for up to 3 years, or longer if the law requires, for dispute handling, safety and legal reasons. Your personal details on these records are removed or anonymised. They are not used for marketing.

Cannot use the app? Request deletion by email

Write to kapniva@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my KapNiva account". Please mention your registered mobile number and whether you want your shopper account, your seller shop, or both deleted. We may ask you to confirm it is you. We aim to complete requests within 7 working days.
