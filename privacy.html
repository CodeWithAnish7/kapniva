Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

KapNiva ("we", "us") is a local fashion marketplace that connects customers with clothing stores. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Customers: mobile number (used for OTP login), name, delivery address, cart, wishlist and order details.
  • Sellers: mobile number, owner name, email, shop name, shop address, shop images, GST number (optional), a photo of the shop and a verification document.
  • Location: some features may ask for location permission. You can deny it and still use the app.

How we use it

  • To log you in, create and deliver orders, and show you shops and products.
  • To verify sellers and prevent fraud, fake orders and misuse.
  • To provide support and respond to your requests.

Who can see your information

  • A seller can see the name, phone number and delivery address of a customer only for orders placed with that seller.
  • Seller verification documents and the shop verification photo are private and visible only to the KapNiva team.
  • Other customers and sellers cannot see your phone number or email.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Service providers

We use trusted services to run the app: Google Firebase (login and database), Supabase (image storage and secure processing), MSG91 (OTP messages) and, when online payments are available, a payment partner. These providers process data only to provide their service to us.

Records and security

Order records are kept for history, support and dispute handling. We protect data with access rules, secure servers and audit logs of important actions. No system is completely risk free, but we work to keep your data safe.

Your choices

  • You can ask us to correct your information or request account help.
  • You can ask about deleting your account. Some order and transaction records may need to be kept for legal and safety reasons.

Children

KapNiva is not meant for children under 18 using the service without a parent or guardian.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be on this page with the updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions, write to kapniva@gmail.com.
