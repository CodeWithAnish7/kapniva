Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

KapNiva ("we", "us") is a local fashion marketplace that connects customers with clothing stores. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Customers: mobile number (used for OTP login), name, delivery address, cart, wishlist and order details.
  • Sellers: mobile number, owner name, email, shop name, shop address, shop images, GST number (optional), a photo of the shop and a verification document.
  • Location: some features may ask for location permission. You can deny it and still use the app.

How we use it

  • To log you in, create and deliver orders, and show you shops and products.
  • To verify sellers and prevent fraud, fake orders and misuse.
  • To provide support and respond to your requests.

Who can see your information

  • A seller can see a customer's name, phone number and delivery address only for orders placed with that seller.
  • For an order you place, you can see the shop name and call the shop's contact number shown on your order page.
  • A seller's email, GST number and verification documents are never shown to customers.
  • Seller verification documents and the shop verification photo are visible only to the KapNiva team.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Microphone and voice search

KapNiva uses your phone's microphone only if you choose voice search. The app asks for microphone permission, and you can say no and still type your search. Your speech is converted to text by the speech recognition service on your phone (this may be provided by your phone's maker or Google). KapNiva does not save your voice recordings.

Location, camera and photos

  • Location: used to help fill your delivery address and show nearby shops. You can deny the permission and type your address yourself.
  • Camera and photos: sellers use them to add shop and product pictures and the verification photo and document. Customers are not required to give camera access.

Service providers

We use trusted services to run the app: Google Firebase (login and database), Supabase (image storage and secure processing), MSG91 (OTP messages) and, when online payments are available, a payment partner. These providers process data only to provide their service to us.

Records and security

Order records are kept for history, support and dispute handling. We protect data with access rules, secure servers and audit logs of important actions. No system is completely risk free, but we work to keep your data safe.

How long we keep your information

  • While your account is active, we keep your account information so the app can work.
  • If you delete your account, we delete your personal information (such as name, address, wishlist and cart).
  • Order and transaction records are kept for up to 3 years, or longer if the law requires, for dispute handling, safety and legal reasons. These records are not used for marketing.

Your choices

  • You can ask us to correct your information or request account help.
  • You can delete your account at any time from inside the app, or by request. See how to delete your account. Some order and transaction records may need to be kept for legal and safety reasons, as described above.

Children

KapNiva is not meant for children under 18 using the service without a parent or guardian.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be on this page with the updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions or complaints, write to kapniva@gmail.com. Grievance contact: KapNiva Support Team, kapniva@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 7 working days.
