Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Cancelling an order

  • Before the seller accepts: you can cancel the order in the app.
  • After the seller accepts: contact support. Cancellation after acceptance is handled case by case.
  • By the seller: a seller may cancel an order, for example if a product is unavailable. The seller must give a reason and you will see the order as cancelled.

Cash on Delivery

For Cash on Delivery orders you pay only when you receive the order. If a COD order is cancelled, nothing was paid, so there is no refund.

Online payments

When online payments are available, a cancelled order that was already paid will be refunded to the original payment method. A refund is marked completed only after our payment partner confirms it. Refund time depends on your bank or payment provider.

Delivered orders

There is no automatic return process in the app yet. If there is a problem with a delivered product, contact support and we will help resolve it with the seller.

Address changes

You can request an address change. Before the seller accepts, it is applied right away. After acceptance, the seller must approve it.

Need help with an order? Write to kapniva@gmail.com with your registered mobile number and order details.
