How to join

Start selling in four steps

Create account

Verify your mobile number and enter your shop details.

Submit proof

Upload a photo of your shop with the board clearly visible and a document that shows your shop is genuine. GST number is optional.

We verify

Our team reviews your details. Your account stays pending until approval.

Start selling

Once approved, add your products and receive orders.

What you need

  • A mobile number for OTP login
  • Shop name, address and owner name
  • A photo taken outside your shop showing the shop board
  • A document that helps us confirm your shop is genuine
  • GST number (optional)

Changed or lost your number?

If your shop is already registered and your mobile number has changed, do not create a new account. Contact support with your shop details and we will help after verification.
